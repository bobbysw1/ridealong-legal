Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

RideAlong is operated by an Australian proprietary company based in Far North Queensland (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information RideAlong collects, how we use it, and who we share it with. It applies to the RideAlong apps for iPhone and Android and to our website, ridealong.info. RideAlong operates in Australia and handles personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information we collect

Account and profile details (name, email, bio, photo), and, if you tell us, your home town and how you heard about RideAlong. Ride, booking, message, review and report content you create, and the reason you give if you cancel a booking or remove a ride (only our staff see that reason). Identity and vehicle verification documents you upload, including a photo that shows your face and, if you choose to send one, a photo of you holding your ID. Vehicle details (description, fuel type, photo, registration document and its expiry date). If you ask to use women-only rides, we record that you asked and whether staff approved it. Device information (such as the type of phone, the app version and when you last opened the app) and, only if you allow it, approximate or precise location. Our servers also record IP addresses for up to a day to limit abuse. We record which version of our terms you accepted and when. Notification preferences and push device tokens. You may also add an optional phone number and home address, which are kept private and are not shown to other users. We record the routes you subscribe to for alerts, recurring-ride schedules you set, and basic usage events (for example, when a ride is posted or a seat is requested) so we can see how the app is used and improve it; usage events are de-identified if you delete your account. The apps also keep a diagnostic log of which screens are opened, app errors, and requests to our servers that fail or are slow, so we can find and fix problems. It does not include your messages or addresses, and it is deleted after 30 days or when you delete your account. If the app crashes or freezes, your device (through Apple’s built-in diagnostics) may send us a technical crash report containing the app version, iOS version, device model and technical call-stack data. Crash reports do not include your name, email or account details and are used only to find and fix faults. If you invite friends, we keep your invite code and a count of the members who joined with it (not who they are); if you join using a code, we record which code you used. We do not store full card numbers or full bank account details.

How we use it

To provide the service, match riders and drivers, process bookings and payments, verify identity and vehicles, keep the community safe, moderate reports, send account and trip notifications, and meet legal obligations. We may also use ride and vehicle details in our own marketing (see Tracking and marketing below).

Payments and payouts (Stripe)

Payments and driver payouts are processed by Stripe. When you pay for a ride, your card details are sent directly to Stripe. When a driver sets up payouts, the details they enter (legal name, date of birth, address, bank account details and a photo of their ID) pass through our servers to Stripe, which verifies and holds them because it is legally required to before it can pay a driver. We do not keep them. Stripe processes this information as an independent provider under its own privacy policy. The identity documents you upload to RideAlong for our own verification are separate from the checks Stripe performs for payouts.

Service providers we share with

We share information only with providers who help us run RideAlong, and only what each needs to do its job: Supabase (application database, file storage and sign-in; our data is hosted in Singapore), Stripe (payments, payouts and payout identity checks), Apple (push notifications, maps and crash diagnostics on iPhone), Google (on Android: push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging, device location services, address search, and Google Maps if you tap for directions), OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap (map tiles on Android and on live-location pages, which means those services see the viewer’s IP address) and Cloudflare (hosting and security for our website, and passing messages you send to support on to our support inbox), Apple iCloud Mail (our support inbox), our email service provider (for sign-in and password emails) and Project OSRM’s public routing service (which receives the start, end and stop points of a trip, but not your name, to work out the driving distance). These providers may store or process information outside Australia, including in Singapore, the United States and other countries. We take reasonable steps to ensure each provider protects personal information to a standard at least equal to this policy and the Australian Privacy Principles. We may also disclose information, including identity documents, to police, courts, regulators or emergency services where the law requires or allows it, to prevent harm, or to protect the safety of our community. We do not sell your personal information.

Tracking and marketing

RideAlong does not show other companies’ ads, does not sell your information, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not share your information with data brokers. Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tags. We may use ride details (such as the route, day, time and seat price) and vehicle details (such as the make, model, colour and photo) in our own marketing, as set out in our Terms of Service. We do not show your name, number plate, phone number, email address or exact pickup or drop-off address in marketing unless you agree first, and you can ask us to stop at any time through Support in the app or support@ridealong.info.

Location

Location is optional. You can browse and enter addresses manually without it. Ride discovery uses your location only when you have allowed it. Trip tracking starts only when you choose to start it and stops when you choose to stop. If you choose to share a live location link, your device uploads its location to our servers about every 12 seconds while sharing is on. We keep only your most recent position (not a history). Anyone with the link can see that position until you stop sharing or it expires after 4 hours; once sharing ends we clear the stored position shortly afterwards and delete the record within a day. The page your contact opens loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which means that service can see your contact’s IP address. RideAlong does not automatically share your location with other users or emergency services, and is not an emergency service.

Identity documents

Identity and vehicle documents are used for manual verification and are kept in private storage that only authorised staff can access. We keep them while your account is open, so we can deal with safety reports, disputes and fraud.

When you delete your account. Vehicle documents are deleted. If you have ever had a booking with another member (as a rider or as a driver), exchanged messages with one, or been part of a safety report, we keep your photo ID (and the photo of you holding it, if you sent one) together with the name, email address and phone number on your account for 12 months after you delete it. This is so that a member cannot avoid a safety report, a dispute or a police investigation by deleting their account. After 12 months they are deleted automatically, unless we need them for longer for an open safety report, a dispute, or a request from police, a court or a regulator. If you never dealt with another member in those ways, your documents are deleted when you delete your account.

Police, courts and regulators. We may give identity documents and account records to police, courts or regulators where the law requires or allows it, including to help investigate a suspected crime or a serious threat to someone’s safety. Only authorised staff can open a kept ID, and each time one is opened it is recorded.

While your account is open you can ask us to delete your documents sooner. Your identity will then need to be checked again before you can book or offer rides, and we may keep the documents where we need them for one of the reasons above.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, tax, safety and dispute-resolution obligations. Account and profile data is kept while your account is active, and deleted or de-identified after your account is closed, except for records we are required to retain (for example, financial and transaction records, which are typically kept for up to seven years under Australian tax law). Vehicle documents are deleted when your account is deleted. Photo ID may be kept for 12 months after that (see Identity documents). If you delete your account we also keep, for staff only, your first name and the reason you gave for leaving.

Keeping information secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls and encryption in transit, and we limit access to authorised people. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to respond promptly if a problem arises.

Access, correction and complaints

You have the right to access and correct the personal information we hold about you. You can do much of this in the app, or contact us at the email below. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information or a privacy complaint, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, change permissions in your device settings, adjust notification preferences, contact support, and request deletion of your account and associated data from within the app (Profile) or by emailing us. See Delete your account.

Where we rely on your permission (for location, notifications, trip tracking or live location sharing), you can withdraw it at any time by turning the feature off in the app or in your device settings. Withdrawing permission stops further collection; it does not affect the rest of your account.

We send emails about your account and your trips. If we send you RideAlong news or offers, you can stop them at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing support@ridealong.info.

Children

RideAlong is not intended for people under 18.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions or requests: support@ridealong.info.
