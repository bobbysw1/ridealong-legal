Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information RideAlong collects, how we use it, and who we share it with. It applies to the RideAlong app and this website. RideAlong operates in Australia and handles personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information we collect

Account and profile details (name, email, bio, photo). Ride, booking, message, review and report content you create. Identity and vehicle verification documents you upload. Device information and, only if you allow it, approximate or precise location. Notification preferences and push device tokens. We do not store full card numbers or full bank account details.

How we use it

To provide the service, match riders and drivers, process bookings and payments, verify identity and vehicles, keep the community safe, moderate reports, send account and trip notifications, and meet legal obligations.

Payments and payouts (Stripe)

Payments and driver payouts are processed by Stripe. When you pay for a ride, your card details are sent directly to Stripe. When a driver sets up payouts, Stripe collects and verifies identity information and bank account details that it is legally required to hold before it can send a driver their earnings. Stripe processes this information as an independent provider under its own privacy policy. The identity documents you upload to RideAlong for our own verification are separate from the checks Stripe performs for payouts.

Service providers we share with

We share information with providers who help us run RideAlong, including Supabase (application data and storage), Stripe (payments and payouts), and Apple (push notifications and maps). We may disclose information where required by law, to prevent harm, or to protect the safety of our community.

Location

Location is optional. You can browse and enter addresses manually without it. Ride discovery uses your location only when you have allowed it. Trip tracking starts only when you choose to start it and stops when you choose to stop. RideAlong does not automatically share your location with other users or emergency services, and is not an emergency service.

Identity documents

Identity and vehicle documents are used for manual verification, are accessible only to authorised reviewers, and are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for review and record keeping, then handled under our retention process.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, change permissions in your device settings, adjust notification preferences, contact support, and request deletion of your account and associated data from within the app (Profile) or by emailing us. See Delete your account.

Children

RideAlong is not intended for people under 18.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions or requests: ridealongappau@gmail.com.
