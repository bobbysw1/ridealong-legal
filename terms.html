Fees and contributions

All amounts are in Australian dollars, shown in full before you confirm. Pay direct: the rider pays RideAlong a $5.50 booking fee per booking, and pays the driver the seat and extras separately. The $5.50 booking fee is non-refundable if you cancel the booking yourself; it is refunded automatically if the driver declines the booking or does not accept it by the scheduled pickup time. Pay securely in app: the rider pays the seat and extras total in the app; RideAlong deducts a 10% service fee from the driver and the driver receives the remaining 90%. Optional extras are charged once per rider who selects them. Seat contributions must reflect genuine shared journey costs.

Payments and payouts (Stripe)

Payments and payouts are handled by Stripe. Card details are sent directly to Stripe and not stored by RideAlong. Secure in-app payments are held by RideAlong until both travellers confirm the trip is complete and both leave a review, after which the driver's share becomes an available balance. Drivers withdraw earnings to their own bank account; a $3.00 fee applies to each standard withdrawal. Where it is available to you, an instant withdrawal to an eligible debit card costs 5% of the amount plus $3.00; the exact fee is shown before you confirm.

Driver payouts and verification

Before withdrawing, a driver must complete a one-time secure verification provided by Stripe that collects identity and bank account details. This is required by law before money can be paid to any person and is separate from RideAlong's own identity check. RideAlong does not store the driver's full bank account details. Drivers agree to Stripe's Connected Account Agreement during that step.

A paid booking that is declined or cancelled by the driver, or a booking that the driver has not accepted by the scheduled pickup time, is automatically refunded to the rider's original payment method. You can also cancel an in-app booking yourself for a full refund up to 2 hours before pickup (the $5.50 booking fee on pay-direct bookings is not refunded if you cancel yourself).
