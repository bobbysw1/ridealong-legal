Fees and contributions

Private drivers set their own seat price, which is a contribution towards the cost of the trip, not a fare. Seat prices are capped at $1 per kilometre of the trip, per seat, and RideAlong will not publish a ride priced above the cap. Where a ride has stops along the way, the price for part of the trip is also capped at $1 per kilometre of that part. Optional extras (luggage, pets, surfboards, child seats) are charged separately and capped at $10 each. An app price limit is not a legal finding that any journey is cost-sharing; drivers remain responsible for that.

All amounts are in Australian dollars, shown in full before you confirm. Pay direct: the rider pays RideAlong a $5.50 booking fee per booking, and pays the driver the seat and extras separately. The $5.50 booking fee is non-refundable if you cancel the booking yourself; it is refunded automatically if the driver declines or cancels the booking or does not accept it by the scheduled pickup time. Pay securely in app: the rider pays the seat and extras total in the app; RideAlong deducts a 10% service fee from the driver and the driver receives the remaining 90%. Optional extras are charged once per rider who selects them. Seat contributions must reflect genuine shared journey costs. Any payment you make to a driver directly is a matter between you and the driver, and RideAlong is not responsible for it.

Founding driver fuel offer

The first 20 drivers whose vehicle is verified by RideAlong receive a discount of 6 cents per litre on fuel at participating BP service stations, for up to 30 litres per day. Places are given in the order vehicles are verified. The discount becomes available once the driver has completed their first trip with RideAlong as the driver (at least one booking on a ride they drove marked as completed). The discount is claimed by showing the barcode in the RideAlong app at the BP counter; it is only shown to eligible drivers, is for the driver's own use and must not be shared or copied. The offer applies to fuel only, has no cash value, and cannot be combined where BP does not allow it. RideAlong may withdraw a driver's access if the barcode is shared or misused, and may end or change the offer at any time, giving drivers who already qualify reasonable notice in the app, unless BP ends or changes its part of the offer.

Payments and payouts (Stripe)

Payments and payouts are handled by Stripe. Card details are sent directly to Stripe and not stored by RideAlong. When a rider pays in the app, RideAlong collects the payment as the driver’s (or business operator’s) limited payment collection agent, and payment to RideAlong counts as payment to the driver. Secure in-app payments are held through our payment provider, Stripe, until the trip is complete, after which the driver's share becomes an available balance. A trip is complete when both travellers confirm it and both leave a review, or automatically 48 hours after the scheduled departure if neither traveller has reported a problem or asked for a refund (we send a reminder after 24 hours). Earnings are only released once the card payment has settled. Drivers withdraw earnings to their own bank account; a $3.00 fee applies to each standard withdrawal. Where it is available to you, an instant withdrawal to an eligible debit card costs 5% of the amount plus $3.00; the exact fee is shown before you confirm.
