Terms of Service

Last updated 6 September 2026

These terms govern your use of RideAlong. By creating an account or using the app you agree to them. RideAlong is a platform that helps people share genuine journeys and split costs. RideAlong is not a taxi, hire-car or emergency service, and does not employ drivers.

Accounts and eligibility

You must be at least 18 and provide accurate information. Drivers must verify their identity and vehicle before offering rides. We may verify, suspend, or remove accounts to protect the community.

Fees and contributions

All amounts are in Australian dollars, shown in full before you confirm. Pay direct: the rider pays RideAlong a $5.50 booking fee per booking, and pays the driver the seat and extras separately. Pay securely in app: the rider pays the seat and extras total in the app; RideAlong deducts a 10% service fee from the driver and the driver receives the remaining 90%. Optional extras are charged once per rider who selects them. Seat contributions must reflect genuine shared journey costs.

Payments and payouts (Stripe)

Payments and payouts are handled by Stripe. Card details are sent directly to Stripe and not stored by RideAlong. Secure in-app payments are held by RideAlong until both travellers confirm the trip is complete and both leave a review, after which the driver's share becomes an available balance. Drivers withdraw earnings to their own bank account; a $2.50 fee applies to each withdrawal.

Driver payouts and verification

Before withdrawing, a driver must complete a one-time secure verification provided by Stripe that collects identity and bank account details. This is required by law before money can be paid to any person and is separate from RideAlong's own identity check. RideAlong does not store the driver's full bank account details. Drivers agree to Stripe's Connected Account Agreement during that step.

Refunds and cancellations

RideAlong is responsible for refunds and card chargebacks to riders. A paid booking that is declined or cancelled, or a ride that is not accepted within 30 minutes of the scheduled pickup, is automatically refunded to the rider's original payment method. Where a refund becomes due after a driver's earnings have already been paid out, RideAlong may recover the amount from the driver's balance or future earnings. These terms do not exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under Australian Consumer Law.

Conduct and safety

Use RideAlong lawfully and respectfully. We may investigate conduct before, during or after a journey, including in messages and at pickup, and may warn, suspend, or permanently remove accounts, including immediately for serious safety concerns. Repeated confirmed late cancellations or no-shows may lead to removal. You can seek a review of a decision except where that would create a safety, legal or investigative risk.

Location and safety features

Location and trip tracking are optional and controlled by you. RideAlong does not automatically share your location and is not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact local authorities.

Tax

RideAlong does not currently add GST to booking or withdrawal fees. Receipts show the actual amount charged. Tax treatment will be updated if RideAlong becomes GST-registered. Drivers are responsible for their own tax obligations.

Liability

RideAlong provides a platform to connect travellers and does not control the conduct of users or the condition of vehicles. To the extent permitted by law, RideAlong is not liable for the acts of users. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or guarantees that cannot be excluded under Australian law.
